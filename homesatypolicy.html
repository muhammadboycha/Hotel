Homestay Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2025

Guest Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy as a guest and are committed to protecting your personal data. Below is an outline of what we collect, how it's used, and your rights.

What Information We Collect

  • Name, contact details, and identification for booking purposes.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Stay preferences and special requests.
  • IP address and browsing activity for security and analytics.

How We Use It

  • To process reservations and payments securely.
  • To personalize your stay experience.
  • To send booking confirmations and updates.
  • To comply with legal and safety obligations.

Your Privacy Rights

As a guest, you can request access to your data, make corrections, or ask us to delete it. You may also opt-out of marketing emails at any time.

Homestay Owner Privacy Policy

We also ensure privacy and security for our homestay partners and owners. This section outlines the data we collect from owners and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Owner name, contact info, and property details.
  • Banking details for payment processing.
  • Usage analytics of the owner dashboard or app.

Usage of Information

  • To list and manage your property on our platform.
  • To process earnings and payouts securely.
  • To communicate important updates or legal requirements.

Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect both owner and guest data. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.
