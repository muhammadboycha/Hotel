Refund Policy

1. Overview

Our refund policy lasts 7 days. If 7 days have passed since your purchase, unfortunately, we can’t offer you a full refund or exchange.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

  • The item must be unused and in the same condition you received it.
  • The item must be in the original packaging.
  • You must provide a receipt or proof of purchase.

3. Non-Refundable Items

Certain types of goods are exempt from being refunded such as perishable items, downloadable software, and gift cards.

4. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven’t received a refund yet, first check your bank account again. Then contact your credit card company, it may take some time before your refund is officially posted. If you've done all of this and still have not received your refund, please contact us at support@yourdomain.com.

5. Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged. If you need to exchange it for the same item, email us at support@yourdomain.com.

6. Shipping Returns

To return your product, you should mail your product to: [Your Return Address]. You will be responsible for paying for your own shipping costs for returning your item. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
