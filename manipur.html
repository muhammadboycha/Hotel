WELCOME TO MANIPUR

Jewel of the East
& Land of Culture

Nestled in the northeastern corner of India, Manipur is a captivating blend of natural beauty, vibrant traditions, and rich cultural heritage. Known for its lush green valleys, tranquil lakes, and the enchanting Loktak Lake with its floating phumdis, Manipur offers an experience like no other. The state is steeped in history and art, from the graceful Manipuri dance to the age-old handloom crafts. Whether it's the colorful festivals, serene landscapes, or warm-hearted people, Manipur promises an unforgettable journey into the heart of India's cultural mosaic.
